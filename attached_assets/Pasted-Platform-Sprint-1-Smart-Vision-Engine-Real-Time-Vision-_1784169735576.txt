Platform Sprint 1 — Smart Vision Engine (Real-Time Vision OS)

Objective

Build @platform/smart-vision into a production-grade, real-time computer vision engine.

This is not a camera component.

This is not a ReadyLoq feature.

This is not a VYBE feature.

This is the reusable vision operating system that every platform application consumes.

The primary design goal is:

The live camera should feel faster, smoother, smarter, and more responsive than the native camera on an iPad or flagship smartphone while remaining portable across all supported devices.

Success Criteria

The finished Smart Vision engine should:
	•	Feel smoother than the default iPad camera during live inspection.
	•	React to movement with minimal perceptible delay.
	•	Maintain stable subject tracking without visible jitter.
	•	Avoid redundant AI inference through Scene Memory.
	•	Use predictive tracking to stay locked on subjects through brief occlusions.
	•	Automatically choose the best capture moment based on quality and policy.
	•	Scale its behavior based on device capabilities and battery state.
	•	Remain completely application-agnostic so that ReadyLoq, VYBE, and future products only provide domain-specific detection models and workflows.

Do not implement any ReadyLoq- or VYBE-specific business logic in this package. Every capability must be reusable across applications.
