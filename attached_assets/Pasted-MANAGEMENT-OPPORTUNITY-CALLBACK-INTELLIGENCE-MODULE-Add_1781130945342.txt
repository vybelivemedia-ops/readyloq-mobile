MANAGEMENT OPPORTUNITY & CALLBACK INTELLIGENCE MODULE

Add a new management-only section to ReadyLoq Command.

This section should focus on:

* Callback reduction
* Opportunity identification
* Property risk intelligence
* Documentation quality

The purpose is to help management identify recurring issues, potential service opportunities, and property risks based on documented findings.

Do NOT position this module as technician monitoring or performance scoring.

CALLBACK INTELLIGENCE

ReadyLoq should track:

Finding
→ Recommendation
→ Follow-Up Visit
→ Outcome

Examples:

Mouse Droppings
→ Bait Placement
→ Follow-Up Inspection
→ Activity Reduced

Utility Penetration
→ No Exclusion Recommendation
→ Callback Occurred

The system should identify patterns that commonly lead to callbacks.

Examples:

* Missing exclusion recommendations
* Undocumented entry points
* Recurring moisture conditions
* Repeated rodent findings

Dashboard Widgets:

* Top Callback Causes
* Most Common Recurring Findings
* Callback Trends by Property Type
* Callback Trends by Pest Type

PROPERTY RISK INSIGHTS

ReadyLoq should analyze findings and identify legitimate property risks.

Examples:

* Utility Penetrations
* Missing Door Sweeps
* Moisture Conditions
* Structural Openings
* Sanitation Issues
* Repeated Rodent Activity
* Repeated Cockroach Activity

Display:

Property Risk Indicators

Property Risk Trends

Recurring Conditions

The goal is to identify conditions that may contribute to future pest activity.

OPPORTUNITY DETECTION

Replace the term “Upsell” with “Opportunity.”

Opportunities must always be based on documented findings and evidence.

Examples:

Finding:
Mouse Droppings + Utility Penetration

Opportunity:
Exclusion Assessment Recommended

Finding:
Repeated Rodent Activity in Trash Room

Opportunity:
Property-Wide Rodent Assessment Recommended

Finding:
Moisture Conditions

Opportunity:
Moisture Correction Recommendation

Finding:
Damaged Door Sweep

Opportunity:
Door Sweep Replacement Recommendation

All opportunities must be supported by:

* Photos
* Findings
* Voice Notes
* Inspection Evidence

UNREPORTED OPPORTUNITY DETECTION

ReadyLoq should identify situations where findings may support additional recommendations that were not documented.

Example:

Finding:
Utility Penetration Documented

Recommendation:
None

ReadyLoq Insight:
Potential exclusion opportunity may exist.

These insights should only be visible to:

* Supervisors
* Branch Managers
* Sales Managers
* Administrators

They should never appear as technician performance warnings.

KNOWLEDGE ENGINE

ReadyLoq should continuously learn relationships between:

* Findings
* Recommendations
* Treatments
* Follow-Up Outcomes
* Callbacks

Future Goal:

Help management identify patterns that reduce callbacks, improve documentation, and create better customer outcomes while generating evidence-based service recommendations.

IMPORTANT DESIGN PRINCIPLE

ReadyLoq should never encourage unnecessary services.

All recommendations and opportunities must be based on documented findings, inspection evidence, and property conditions.

The platform exists to improve pest management outcomes, documentation quality, and customer communication.
