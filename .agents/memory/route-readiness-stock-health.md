---
name: Route Readiness & Truck Stock Health (proactive inventory signals)
description: Design decisions behind the two proactive stock-projection signals layered on top of the reactive restockThreshold alert.
---

**Consumption-rate calculations must read from `inventoryTransactionsTable` (`truck_deduction` rows), never from `treatmentLogsTable.totalProductOz`.** `totalProductOz` is always fl-oz regardless of the product's actual tracked unit, so any product not measured in fl-oz would silently get a fabricated number if averaged from that column. The ledger's `quantity`/`unit` pair is unit-consistent with the truck balance it fed. **Why:** treatment logs exist for compliance/billing narrative, not for unit-safe quantity math — reusing them for consumption averages was the obvious-but-wrong first approach. **How to apply:** when a product's unit doesn't support ledger-based averaging (no matching history), surface "insufficient-data" rather than falling back to treatment-log math.

**Reversals net out by joining `truck_deduction_reversal` rows back to their original `truck_deduction` via shared `referenceId`, not by date-window exclusion.** A deduction that gets reversed (e.g. a deleted treatment log) must not count toward average daily consumption. **How to apply:** any new consumption/average helper over the ledger must subtract reversed quantities keyed by `referenceId`, not just filter on `movementType = 'truck_deduction'`.

**Two proactive inventory signals are deliberately kept separate, never blended into one score:** "Today's Route Readiness" (will today's already-scheduled stops exceed on-hand, using avg usage per stop × stop count) is TODAY-scoped and reads `technicianDailyRigsTable` + inspections for the calendar day. "Truck Stock Health" (days-of-coverage = on-hand ÷ avg daily consumption, vs. configured `replenishmentTargetsTable` window) is a longer-range trend, independent of today's schedule. **Why:** a stop that's short today and a truck that's merely trending toward a future shortage are different signals a technician/manager needs to act on differently — collapsing them loses that distinction. Keep this split when extending either feature.

**Local-calendar-date/timezone helpers (`parseTzOffsetMinutes`, `localCalendarDate`, `localMidnightUtc`, `localEndOfDayUtc`) live in a shared `lib/calendar-tz.ts`, not duplicated per route file.** Any new endpoint that needs "what day is it for this technician" must import from there, not reimplement the offset math inline (this was extracted out of `schedule.ts` for reuse in `daily-rig.ts`).
