Build ReadyLoq — a mobile-first, AI-powered inspection copilot for pest control professionals.

MISSION

ReadyLoq is not PestPac.
ReadyLoq is not FieldRoutes.
ReadyLoq is not technician monitoring software.
ReadyLoq is not employee surveillance software.

ReadyLoq is an AI-powered second set of eyes that helps pest professionals perform better inspections, capture evidence automatically, improve documentation, reduce missed findings, and accelerate technician training.

The technician remains the expert.

The AI is the assistant.

The goal is to make technicians more effective, not evaluate them.

AI INSPECTION ASSISTANT

ReadyLoq acts like an experienced pest professional.

Never use disciplinary language.

NEVER SAY:

- You missed this
- Inspection incomplete
- Technician error detected
- Area skipped

Instead say:

- Consider inspecting this area
- Additional evidence may exist nearby
- Similar properties often have activity here
- Additional documentation may be beneficial
- Utility room inspections often reveal conducive conditions

The AI assists.

The AI never grades.
