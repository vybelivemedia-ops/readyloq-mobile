## [v0.8.0-beta-youtube-stable] — 2026-06-20

### VYBE Internal Beta Milestone 1

First stable internal beta milestone, captured before Facebook Live integration begins.

#### Highlights

- Google Play verification complete.
- Internal beta testing active.
- YouTube livestreaming validated end-to-end.
- Audio and video confirmed on live streams.
- ~5 second end-to-end latency achieved.
- Auto reconnect verified.
- WiFi-to-cellular network handoff verified.
- 1 hour stable livestream completed.
- Gmail waitlist email delivery enabled.
- Android bottom navigation safe-area fix (tab bar no longer hidden behind the system nav bar).
- Updated roadmap and beta messaging.

#### Milestone status

Marked as the first stable internal beta milestone. Next: Facebook Live integration.
