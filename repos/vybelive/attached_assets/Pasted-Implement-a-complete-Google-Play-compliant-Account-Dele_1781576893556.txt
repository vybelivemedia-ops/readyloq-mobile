Implement a complete Google Play compliant Account Deletion and Account Lifecycle Management system for VYBE LIVE.

ACCOUNT DELETION PAGE

Create a public webpage:

https://getvybe.live/delete-account

Requirements:

* Publicly accessible without login.
* Linked from website footer.
* Linked from Privacy Policy.
* Explain account deletion process.
* Explain what data is deleted.
* Explain any retention periods.
* Include deletion request form.

Form Fields:

* Email
* Username
* Optional reason

After submission:

* Save request to database.
* Email [admin@getvybe.live](mailto:admin@getvybe.live).
* Display confirmation message.

ACCOUNT DELETION INSIDE APP

Add:

Settings → Account → Delete Account

DELETION FLOW

STEP 1

Display warning:

"Are you sure you want to delete your VYBE LIVE account?

This will permanently remove your profile, videos, livestream history, messages, followers, following lists, account information, and associated personal data.

This action cannot be undone."

Buttons:

* Cancel
* Continue

STEP 2

Display second confirmation:

"Final Confirmation

You are about to permanently delete your VYBE LIVE account.

This action is irreversible."

Require:

☐ Checkbox:
"I understand my account and associated data will be permanently deleted."

☐ User must type:
DELETE

Buttons:

* Cancel
* Continue

STEP 3

Require password verification.

STEP 4

Display:

"Your deletion request has been received. Your account has been scheduled for deletion."

Immediately:

* Log user out.
* Revoke active sessions.
* Disable account access.
* Set account status = PENDING_DELETION.

ACCOUNT DELETION TIMELINE

Day 0:

* User submits deletion request.
* Account disabled immediately.
* User cannot access account.

Day 25:

* Send warning email.

Subject:
Final Reminder: Your VYBE LIVE Account Will Be Permanently Deleted

Body:
Your account is scheduled for permanent deletion in 5 days. If this request was made in error, contact support immediately.

Day 30:

* Permanently delete account.
* Delete profile.
* Delete authentication credentials.
* Delete messages where applicable.
* Delete user content where applicable.
* Remove personal information.
* Record deletion timestamp.

ACCOUNT INACTIVITY MANAGEMENT

Track:

* Last login
* Last app activity
* Last stream
* Last upload

Inactive Account Definition:

* No activity for 180 consecutive days.

INACTIVITY WARNING

At 175 days:

Send email:

Subject:
Your VYBE LIVE Account Will Be Suspended Soon

Body:
Your account has been inactive for nearly 180 days. Sign in within the next 5 days to keep your account active.

If available:

* Send in-app notification.

ACCOUNT SUSPENSION

At 180 days:

* Suspend account.
* Disable login.
* Preserve all account data.
* Set status = SUSPENDED.

Send email:

Subject:
Your VYBE LIVE Account Has Been Suspended

Body:
Your account has been suspended due to inactivity. You have 30 days to reactivate your account by signing in.

ACCOUNT RECOVERY PERIOD

Days 180-210:

* User can reactivate account.
* Restore full access.
* Remove suspension status.

PERMANENT DELETION AFTER SUSPENSION

Day 205:

Send final warning email.

Subject:
Final Notice: Your VYBE LIVE Account Will Be Deleted

Body:
Your suspended account will be permanently deleted in 5 days due to inactivity. Sign in now to reactivate your account.

Day 210:

Automatically:

* Delete account.
* Delete personal information.
* Delete profile.
* Delete authentication data.
* Delete user-generated content where applicable.
* Record deletion timestamp.
