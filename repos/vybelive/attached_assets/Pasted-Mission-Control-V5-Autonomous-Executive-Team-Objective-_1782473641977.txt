Mission Control V5 – Autonomous Executive Team

Objective

Transform the existing AI executives from advisors into autonomous executives that can plan, coordinate, execute approved work, and collaborate with one another.

The executives should no longer simply answer questions.

They should actively manage work inside Mission Control.

⸻

Executive State Machine

Every executive must always be in one of these states.

🟢 Idle

Waiting for work.

🟡 Planning

Breaking work into tasks.

🔵 Working

Performing inspections or assigned work.

🟣 Collaborating

Waiting for another executive.

🔴 Needs Approval

Waiting for founder approval.

Display each executive’s current state in Headquarters.

⸻

Chief of Staff

The Chief of Staff becomes the orchestrator of the executive team.

Responsibilities:

Receive founder requests.

Determine objectives.

Break work into executive tasks.

Assign work.

Track progress.

Resolve dependencies.

Collect reports.

Deliver one executive briefing.

The Chief never performs specialist work.

The Chief delegates.

⸻
