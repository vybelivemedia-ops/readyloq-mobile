VYBE AI CLIP ENGINE - REAL RECOGNITION REQUIRED

Do not build simulated voice commands or simulated gesture recognition.

Build real recognition from the start.

Requirements:

1. Real Voice Recognition

* Continuously listen during live streams.
* Detect creator-defined trigger phrases.
* Default phrases:
    * “VYBE clip that”
    * “VYBE highlight”
    * “Save that”
    * “Mark moment”
    * “Create clip”
* Must work hands-free while streaming.
* Must create timeline markers instantly.

2. Real Hand Gesture Recognition

* Use live camera frames from POV mode.
* Detect:
    * Thumbs up
    * Peace sign
    * Pointing gesture
    * Open palm
    * Custom gestures later
* Detection should generate timeline markers instantly.

3. Context-Aware AI
    The AI should not rely solely on gestures or voice.

Analyze:

* Voice excitement
* Volume spikes
* Laughter
* Crowd reactions
* Sudden motion
* Viewer spikes
* Chat spikes
* Repeated objects/persons
* Speech-to-text content

4. Creator Learning Engine
    Learn from:

* Clips creators save
* Clips creators delete
* Clips creators publish
* Clips with highest watch time
* Clips with highest engagement

Generate a creator preference profile.

5. Universal Learning Engine
    Aggregate anonymized creator behavior.

Learn:

* What moments become reels
* What moments become shorts
* What moments become stories
* Which gestures correlate with successful clips
* Which voice commands correlate with successful clips

6. AI Highlight Generation
    Automatically generate:

* Highlights
* Reels
* Shorts
* Stories

Determine:

* Start frame
* End frame
* Aspect ratio
* Zoom and crop
* Best duration

7. Creator Review Layer
    Nothing posts automatically.

Creator can:

* Preview
* Edit
* Trim
* Approve
* Delete

before publishing.

8. Recording Foundation
    All streams must be recorded locally and/or cloud archived.

AI clipping cannot be reliable without access to the full stream recording.

Priority Order:

P1:

* Stream recording
* Archive management
* Delete streams
* Rename streams

P2:

* Real voice recognition
* Real gesture recognition

P3:

* AI highlights
* AI clips
* Learning engine

Goal:

A creator should be able to stream for hours without touching their phone and still receive ready-to-post clips generated from real voice and gesture signals.
