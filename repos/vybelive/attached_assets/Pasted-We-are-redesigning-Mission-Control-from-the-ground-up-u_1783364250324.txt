Prompt for Replit

Build the centerpiece of Mission Control as an original 2D interactive headquarters.

Do NOT build a 3D environment.

Do NOT create a game.

This is a professional executive dashboard.

Headquarters Concept

The headquarters is shaped like the VYBE “V”.

The V itself is the building.

Every executive occupies a room inside one branch of the V.

The Founder overlooks the entire headquarters.

Agent Zero powers the headquarters from its core.

Quinn operates from the command bridge near the center.

Interaction

Clicking a room opens that executive.

Room lights animate when work is active.

Idle rooms dim slightly.

Blocked rooms pulse softly red.

Completed objectives briefly glow green.

Agent Zero pulses while executing tasks.

Quinn’s room displays live conversations.

The Founder can zoom between:
	•	Entire headquarters
	•	Executive level
	•	Individual room

Requirements

Keep the headquarters flat (2D).

No simulated walking.

No avatars.

No unnecessary animations.

Focus on clarity, responsiveness, and usability.

The headquarters should become the signature visual identity of Mission Control while remaining fast enough for daily operational use on desktop, tablet, and mobile.

These two phases give Replit a clear separation between building the design system and building the interactive Mission Control experience, making implementation much easier and more maintainable.
