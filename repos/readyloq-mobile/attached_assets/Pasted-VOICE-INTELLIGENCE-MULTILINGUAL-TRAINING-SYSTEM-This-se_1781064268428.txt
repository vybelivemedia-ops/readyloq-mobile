VOICE INTELLIGENCE & MULTILINGUAL TRAINING SYSTEM

This section expands ReadyLoq's capabilities beyond simple voice notes.

ReadyLoq should become an intelligent pest control language and training engine that understands how technicians actually speak in the field.

VOICE-FIRST INSPECTION WORKFLOW

Technicians should be encouraged to inspect naturally without typing.

Examples:

"Fresh mouse droppings behind refrigerator."

"Heavy German roach activity under prep sink."

"Looks like old activity."

"Missing door sweep at rear exit."

"Gap around pipe penetration."

"Possible moisture issue behind water heater."

ReadyLoq should:

- Convert speech to text
- Identify pest terminology
- Extract structured findings
- Create inspection notes
- Associate notes with evidence
- Suggest professional terminology

SMART TERMINOLOGY CONVERSION

ReadyLoq should convert casual technician language into professional report language.

Example:

Technician Says:
"Little black stuff behind stove."

ReadyLoq Suggests:
Possible Mouse Droppings

Technician Confirms

Report Generates:
Mouse droppings observed behind stove.

Example:

Technician Says:
"Hole around pipe."

ReadyLoq Suggests:
Utility Penetration

Report Generates:
Unsealed utility penetration observed.
