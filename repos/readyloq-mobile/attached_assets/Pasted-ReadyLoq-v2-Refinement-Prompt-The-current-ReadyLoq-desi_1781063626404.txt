FINAL PRODUCT VISION

ReadyLoq should feel like:

A professional pest technician’s body camera, inspection assistant, evidence recorder, and report writer.

It should not feel like:

Management software
Employee monitoring software
Traditional pest control software

The technician should feel that ReadyLoq helps them find, document, and explain what they see while reducing paperwork and improving inspection quality.
